How to play

  1. Swipe or drag anywhere on the board, or use the arrow keys, to slide every tile.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one and double, adding to your score.
  3. Every move drops in a new tile. If the board fills with no merges left, the round ends.
  4. Aim for 2048. There is no undo, so a move cannot be taken back.

Install

Install Merge for an app-like window and offline play.

Phone says Merge is installed but won’t open? Remove the old app from your Home Screen or browser app settings, then try again. The phone controls the bottom system bar, so it keeps the colour it was given when the app was installed.

About & privacy

No accounts, ads or tracking. Your board, best scores and appearance choices stay on this device. Your light/dark choice is also remembered in a first-party cookie so an installed app can choose matching launch chrome. “All settings” clears the saved board and appearance choices; best scores have their own reset.
