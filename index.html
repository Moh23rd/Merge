How to play

  1. Swipe anywhere on the board, or use the arrow keys, to slide every tile.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one and double, adding to your score.
  3. Every move drops in a new tile. If the board fills with no merges left, the round ends.
  4. Aim for 2048. There is no undo, so a move cannot be taken back.

Install

Install Merge for an app-like window and offline play.

About & privacy

No accounts, ads or tracking. Your board, best scores and appearance choices stay on this device. Theme colours are also remembered in two first-party cookies so an installed app can match them. “All settings” clears the saved board and appearance choices; best scores have their own reset.
