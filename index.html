Merge board

Pick a board size. Changing it starts a new round.

3 × 3 is a quick warm-up, 4 × 4 is the standard, and 5 × 5 is the long game. Each size keeps its own best score.

Reset this game?

Only this game’s saved progress will be cleared.

Your look and every other game stay exactly as they are.

How to play

  1. Swipe or drag anywhere on the board, or use the arrow keys, to slide every tile.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one and double, adding to your score.
  3. Every move drops in a new tile. If the board fills with no merges left, the round ends.
  4. Aim for 2048. There is no undo, so a move cannot be taken back.

Your board is saved as you play — reopen the app and pick up where you left off.

About Sixfold

Install

Install Sixfold for an app-like window and offline play.

Already installed but it won’t open? Remove the old app from your Home Screen or browser app settings, then try again. The phone controls the bottom system bar, so it keeps the colour it was given when the app was installed.

About & privacy

No accounts, ads or tracking. Your board, best scores and appearance choices stay on this device. Each game has its own reset in its tool row, so clearing one game never touches the others. Your light/dark choice is also remembered in a first-party cookie so an installed app can choose matching launch chrome.
