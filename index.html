How to play

  1. Swipe or drag anywhere on the board, or use the arrow keys, to slide every tile.
  2. Two tiles with the same number merge into one and double, adding to your score.
  3. Every move drops in a new tile. If the board fills with no merges left, the round ends.
  4. Aim for 2048. There is no undo, so a move cannot be taken back.

Your board is saved as you play — reopen the app and pick up where you left off.

About Sixfold

Install

Install Sixfold for an app-like window and offline play.

Already installed but it won’t open? Remove the old app from your Home Screen or browser app settings, then try again. The phone controls the bottom system bar, so it keeps the colour it was given when the app was installed.

About & privacy

No accounts, ads or tracking. Your board, best scores and appearance choices stay on this device. Each game has its own reset in its tool row, so clearing one game never touches the others. Your light/dark choice is also remembered in a first-party cookie so an installed app can choose matching launch chrome.

Technique guide

Short, repeatable moves. Use Auto notes to fill candidates, then look for these patterns.

  • Naked single

    One empty cell accepts only one digit. Every other digit already appears in its row, column or box.

  • Hidden single

    Within a row, column or box, a digit fits in only one cell. The cell may have other candidates, but this digit has nowhere else to go.

  • Naked pair

    Two cells in one unit contain the same two candidates. Those two digits can be removed from the other cells in that unit.

  • Pointing pair

    Inside a box, a digit’s candidates sit in one row or column. Remove that digit from the rest of that row or column.

  • Box-line reduction

    In a row or column, a digit’s candidates sit inside one box. Remove that digit from the other cells of that box.

  • X-Wing

    Two rows both confine a digit to the same two columns. Those columns can lose that digit everywhere else. The same idea works with two columns and two rows.

When no simple single is available, Hint will still keep you moving and say that it is revealing a cell.
